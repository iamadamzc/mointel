HOW IT WORKS

Two engines. Deliberately never merged.

One reads the tape. One reads the filings. The desk shows both rather than combining them into one number.

DEMAND — THE TAPE

What the market is doing

A continuous read of the market. Volume against a twenty-session baseline, the ranking that follows from it, and the moment a name enters the day's discovery list.

STRUCTURE — THE PAPERWORK

What that move is standing on

Float, and whether the float can be trusted. Warrants, convertibles, PIPEs, ATMs, resale shelves, reverse-split distortion. The levels, and the warnings attached to them.

WHEN THERE IS NOTHING TO SAY

Unknown stays unknown

Nothing missing is rounded to zero, and nothing is asserted that the source did not state. Where the research has nothing, the desk prints nothing and says so.

BOUNDARIES

What it is not.

Not a scanner

It does not run your filters or replace a scanner. It ranks names that are already moving.

Not a broker

No account, no orders, no positions, no connection to your brokerage of any kind.

Not signals

No entry arrows, no exit alerts, no chat room. It reports; it does not recommend.

Not advice

Not a broker-dealer, not an investment adviser, not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
