HOW IT WORKS

Two engines. Deliberately never merged.

One reads the tape. One reads the filings. The desk shows you both rather than averaging them into a score.

DEMAND — THE TAPE

What the market is doing

One long-lived connection to the consolidated feed. Cumulative volume, relative volume against a twenty-session baseline, live ranking, and the moment a name enters the day's discovery list.

STRUCTURE — THE PAPERWORK

What that move is standing on

Float, and whether the float can be trusted. Warrants, convertibles, PIPEs, ATMs, resale shelves, reverse-split distortion. The levels, and the warnings attached to them.

THE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST

Unknown stays unknown

Nothing missing is rounded to zero, and nothing is asserted that the source did not state. Where the research has nothing, the desk prints nothing and says so.
