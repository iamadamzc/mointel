HOW IT WORKS

Two engines. Deliberately never merged.

One reads the tape. One reads the filings. They answer different questions, they disagree often, and the desk shows you both rather than averaging them into a score.

DEMAND — THE TAPE

What the market is doing

One long-lived connection to the consolidated feed, held open all session. Cumulative volume, relative volume against a twenty-session baseline, live ranking, and the moment a name crosses into the day's discovery list.

STRUCTURE — THE PAPERWORK

What that move is standing on

Float, and whether the float can be trusted. Supply waiting in the wings: warrants, convertibles, PIPEs, ATMs, resale shelves, reverse-split distortion. The levels that matter and the warnings attached to them.

THE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST

Unknown stays unknown

No missing value is quietly rounded to zero, and no classification is asserted that the source did not state. When the tape has found something and the research has nothing to say about it, the desk prints exactly that and leaves the column empty.

BOUNDARIES

What it is not.

Four things this product does not do, stated here rather than discovered on day one.

Not a scanner

It does not filter the universe or replace the one you run. It works on the names a scan has already surfaced, and goes further than one can.

Not a broker

No account, no orders, no positions, no connection of any kind to your brokerage. It cannot place a trade and never asks for permission to.

Not signals

No entry arrows, no exit alerts, no chat room. It gives you what a decision needs; the decision stays yours.

Not advice

An information service about publicly traded securities. Not a broker-dealer, not an investment adviser, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
