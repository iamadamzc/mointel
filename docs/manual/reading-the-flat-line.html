Why it reads as nothing

Two things stack up, and both are mine.

1. A silent row is drawn in the faintest ink on the desk. When every minute in the window is zero, the bars switch to ink-3 at 50% opacity — dimmer than an ordinary row. I did that so a quiet minute would not shout. The effect is that the encoding says ignore me at exactly the moment the data says look at me: a name that did 107× normal volume and has now stopped.

2. The whole dead range is one and a half pixels tall — and that part is deliberate. Full height is the active line at 300 trades/min. The dead line is 30. On a 14px band that puts every value from 0 to 30 inside the bottom 1.4px, so dead and nearly dead and zero render as one hairline.

That compression is the price of a property worth keeping. The scale is anchored to your active line rather than to each row's own peak, so bar heights compare down the column. Normalise per row and a dead name and a raging one both fill their box — a board of identical-looking charts carrying different magnitudes. Any rescaling that separated 0 from 29 would cost that. And 0 and 25 trades a minute are the same fact at your size: both untradeable, which is why the verdict already treats them as one. So the fix makes the hairline visible rather than making it bigger. The band that carries the information is 30–300, and it has 90% of the pixels.

The proposed column changes one thing and adds one: silent rows keep normal ink weight instead of being dimmed, and the bars sit on a visible baseline. Zero then reads as a value resting on a floor rather than as an empty cell. Same data, same scale, same geometry — only the encoding.

What each state means

Trading now. At or above 300/min, your active line.
Fading. Between the dead line and the active line — the verdict holds rather than flipping, so this is the band where a name is on its way somewhere.
Measured, and quiet. Every minute counted, every minute zero. Beside a large RVOL this is the most useful row on the board: it ran, and it is over.
— Not measured. No minute data reached the desk. Never drawn as zero, because a measurement of nothing and no measurement are different facts.
