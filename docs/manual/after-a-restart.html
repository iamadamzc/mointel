After a Restart

A restart used to leave a name's session high reading lower than the price that actually printed. Nothing was lost and nothing was overwritten — the desk rebuilt itself from sources that had never seen the print. The worst of it was in premarket, which is the half of the session the rebuild had no source for at all.

What the rebuild could see, and what it could not

When the desk restarts mid-session it has to reconstruct what it already knew. It had three sources for that, and between them they left a hole.

The daily bar is the regular session plus the closing cross — 09:30 to 16:00 and nothing outside it. The evening rebuild only runs once the bell has gone. The minute backfill does reach back to 04:00, but only for the focused set, and that is 200 names plus your pins out of a candidate universe several times larger. For every other name, premarket was covered by nothing at all.

Measured on 2026-09-18, after a restart at 21:05Z. 1,481 of 7,936 rows carried a session high below the one that had printed before the restart. 1,160 of those highs — the large majority — had printed before 09:30.

The low half of the session had the same defect, for the same reason and in the same places.

Why a wrong number is worse than a missing one

Nothing was lost and nothing was lowered. The engine came back and rebuilt itself honestly from what it could reach, and what it could reach had never seen the premarket print. That is the problem.

a low high Reads as an observation. The board says the name got to $4.10. You have no way to tell that from a name that genuinely got to $4.10, and the level you would trade against is wrong.
no high at all Reads as absent, which is true and checkable. Everywhere else on this desk a missing measurement is drawn as missing, never as zero, for exactly this reason.
NO_PRINT The dangerous one. A name that traded before a restart and not after used to read as a positive assertion that the market did not trade — a claim about the tape, produced by a process lifecycle event.

What changed

The desk already writes every minute bar to disk as it goes, so the session's own tape was never missing — it was simply not being read back. On restart the engine now replays those stored bars through its own ingest path first, before it seeds anything from the vendor snapshot. Premarket is recovered for every symbol that has bars on disk, not just the focused 200.

It replays once, before the live stream starts, and it discards the events those bars would have raised. A restart at 14:00 must not re-announce every new high since 04:00 to someone who watched them arrive the first time.

The order is the whole of the fix

This is the part that is easy to get backwards, and it was got backwards in writing before anyone ran it.

The snapshot seed tells the engine how much volume is already accounted for, and up to which minute. The engine then discards any bar at or before that minute — correctly, because its volume is already counted. But the bar is discarded whole, and its high goes with it.

So a replay that runs after the seed throws away most of the session and rebuilds the very hole it exists to close, while cheerfully reporting how many bars it fed. Run it before the seed and the bars land normally; the seed then takes back their volume and the high survives, because a high is kept by taking the larger of the two rather than by adding.

Which source owns which number

The two mechanisms are not layers, and the question of which one wins was settled by measuring rather than by arguing. They were never competing — they own different fields.

volume → snapshot The vendor's daily volume is not regular-session-only, so it already counts the whole day. Measured at 3 of 2,747 rows, 0.1%. Summing the local minutes would only be as complete as the local minutes.
high, low → replay The same bar's high IS regular-session-only. One bar, two windows. The daily high simply cannot supply an extreme that printed at 07:40, so the stored tape has to.

What this still does not fix

A name with no stored bars is untouched by replay and takes whatever the seed gives it. If the desk was not running when that name printed its premarket high, nothing on this disk saw it and nothing here invents it. That is the same direction as every other reconstruction on this desk: absent beats plausible.

The number that closes it

The mechanism is live. What settles whether it worked is one count taken after a real restart: rows whose session high reads below the pre-restart value. It was 1,481 on 2026-09-18 and it should be zero.

The two counts have to be taken over the same population or they do not compare. A name with no stored bars can still read low without the fix being wrong — that is the exclusion above, working as intended. So the comparison must be restricted to symbols that have stored premarket bars, or the denominators are describing different things and a real success will look like a partial one.
