# The voice reference: Warrior Trading

**His answer, 2026-09-15, verbatim:** *"https://www.warriortrading.com/ this is
the most successful and actually the trading style this app is patterned after.
they offer scanners and charts. i would like tmi to be complementary."*

Read by Marketing the same day, home page only, in the in-app browser.

## What the reference is not

The home page is a sales page. Profit figures on named members, a founder
total, testimonials, success stories, coupon codes, a chat room, courses, a
"join now". **It is the register he rejected on the first landing page** and
every prohibition in the charter forbids it: no outcome claims, no proof, no
community. **Nothing in its prose is to be copied.**

## What the reference is

**The reader.** A Warrior Trading student is who lands on this page. That is
worth more than a style, because recognition is the failure the page has been
fighting and recognition is vocabulary used correctly. A Warrior student already
knows, and will notice if the page does not:

- the five pillars, and float under 10M as the one that matters most
- gappers, the top-gapper list, the 07:00 to 10:00 window, high of day
- micro pullback, flat top, ABCD, VWAP reclaim, the first candle to make a new
  high
- float rotation, "the float has rotated", offerings and reverse splits as the
  thing that kills a name
- the scanner as the first thing on the screen every morning, and the chart as
  the second

**The page already speaks some of this** (ABCD, float, premarket, the shelf that
went effective on Tuesday). Where it uses an engine's word instead of the
student's, that is the fix. `docs/marketing/what-it-is-not.md` item 1 stands:
we do not say "finds", because their scanner finds.

**The relation.** *Complementary.* Their scanner finds the gapper and their chart
shows the move. TMI says what the move is standing on. That is the supplement
relationship the desk retired as a *hook* ("Your scanner finds it. This tells
you what it is.") and it has now been stated by him as a *position*. Whether it
gets restated on the page, and how, is Commercial's to rule; routed 2026-09-15.
Until then the Boundaries card carries it: *"It does not run your filters or
replace a scanner."*

## What changes in the copy, and what does not

- **Does not change:** the prohibitions, the H1, the registered statement, the
  research section he called fine.
- **Changes, on the next pass:** any place a Warrior student would use a
  different word. Candidates: "the day's runners" is fine; "in play" is fine;
  "cohort" was already cut. The 07:00 to 10:00 window is Cameron's, and now
  that the reader is Cameron's student it is *their* window; whether it goes
  back on the page is a question for him, not a fix.
- **Never:** "join", "community", "learn", "members", a profit figure. Those
  are the reference's words and they are the ones we cannot have.

## The test, restated

A Warrior student reads the page and thinks: this was written by someone in the
same chat at 07:15, looking at the same gapper list, who went and read the
filing.
