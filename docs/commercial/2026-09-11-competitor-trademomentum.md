## 3. THE COMMERCIAL READ

**What he charges.** *"$147/mo – 7 Day Free Trial"*, *"Cancel anytime"*,
*"No Contracts"* (pricing page and trial page). An annual plan exists — the
storefront redirect lands on a Whop page whose slug is *full-access-annual*
— **and its price is behind a login; not established.** A *"60 Day Trading
Bootcamp"* is by application, *"Payment plans available"*, price unpublished.
Scale markers on his own pages: *"10,000+ Students Taught"*, *"Thousands of
traders in the room each morning"*, Whop 4.8★ (400+), Trustpilot 4.7★ (80+);
YouTube 18.3K subscribers. The pitch for paying is the room and the alerts —
*"watch me do it every single day"* — not the list. The list is free because
it is the advertisement.

**What the video is, commercially.** Not a pricing event. It names no price
and no date, it addresses a commenter who blamed the list for losses, and it
swipes at *"copycats"*. It is a free-to-paid funnel post, and the free series
resumed the same afternoon. If anything changes, it will be visible in the
channel listing before it is visible anywhere else; nothing in it changed
between 2026-08-20 and 2026-09-11.

**Is it the same job as ours? No — and the difference is the desk's own
registered boundary.** His product picks names and gives entries: a trigger,
a confirmation, targets, a chase zone. That is a **signal product with a
personality**, sold to beginners (*"little Timmy in my comments"*), and its
proof claims — *"caught the top stock Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday"*, *"I
nailed the literal BOTTOM entry"*, *"I predicted the PERFECT squeeze"* — are
the class of claim this desk's Marketing charter prohibits and §2f rules out.
The desk's registered statement says *"not a scanner and it is not a signal
service"*, its architecture lists buy/sell signals as a standing non-goal,
and it does not pick. **So "track the same thing" splits into two parts:**

- **The part the desk already computes, and could make a bigger deal of** —
  the per-name levels his game plan is built from (the prior day's high, VWAP,
  the prior range), the continuity across days (*"a familiar face from
  earlier this week"* is the cycle read), and the theme or catalyst behind a
  run, which he asserts and the desk grades. His list is three names with
  chart levels and no catalyst, float, structure or supply; the desk's board
  carries all four per row and says when it has none.
- **The part the desk has ruled out** — the entry trigger, the confirmation,
  the targets, the *"no break, no trade"* instruction. Those are signals.
  Adopting them would move the product across the boundary he chose when he
  named it.

**Does it change this seat's read of where the product sits? It sharpens it
rather than moving it.** This is the first published price in the desk's
own audience for a retail momentum product: $147 a month buys a live room,
ten-plus alerts a day and the lists, from a channel with 18K subscribers.
That is a data point for pricing the gates map has never had, and it prices
the *opposite* product — the one that tells you what to buy. The desk's
differentiator against it is the one the trader already ruled: depth on the
names that are moving, with the reasons and the structure, and honesty about
what it does not know. The overlap is the artefact shape — a nightly and a
premarket per-name read — not the content.

**Not done here, by the brief:** no recommendation on what to build (Research
and Design, routed by Integration); no marketing angle (Marketing's sentence
to write); nothing that names him on the page (§2d, unchanged).
