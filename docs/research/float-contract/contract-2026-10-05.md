## 1. What the number is

    bound = filed share count (shares)  minus  insider direct holdings (shares), both as of ONE date

A number the desk COMPUTES from SEC filings. It is an upper bound on the float only when the inputs line up (section 3); the cell says so conditionally. It is not a float, not a vendor figure, and it makes no claim about what trades.

**Insider set (settled, Commercial 5b):** every reporting owner on a SINGLE-owner Form 3, 4 or 5, any role (director, officer, 10 percent owner, "other"); direct (`D`) non-derivative common or ordinary shares only; a joint filing is not attributed (it can only leave the number higher). The 13D/13G half is OUT: those amounts are beneficial ownership (Rule 13d-3: includes the right to acquire within 60 days, and voting or investment control) and put WCT below zero.

## 2. The change from the harness that this contract requires (a defect found reading `run.ts`)

The harness picks, per owner, the LATEST filing by FILING date and checks only that no split lies between the filing date and the count date (`run.ts` arm (a), `latestByOwner`). **It never requires the holding to be dated at or before the count.** A director granted shares after the count date, whose Form 4 is filed later, is subtracted from a count that does not contain those shares. That moves the bound too LOW, the dangerous direction, and it is the case Commercial named ("an insider's holding dated after the count"). The 10-name run could not show it (the insider-only half was small and most holdings were refused for splits), which is not evidence it cannot happen. I have read the code for this; I have not run a name through it to show a wrong number.

**Contract rule (as-of alignment):** per owner, use the holding with the latest AS-OF date that is at or before the count's as-of date. As-of date of a holding: for Form 4, the `transactionDate` of the last non-derivative direct common row on or before the count date, with that row's `sharesOwnedFollowingTransaction`; for Form 3 and Form 5, `periodOfReport`. A filing made after the count date is still eligible if its as-of date is on or before it (a late Form 4). An owner with no eligible holding is NOT subtracted (the bound stays higher; safe). An owner whose only eligible holding is older than the filing window (730 days) is likewise not subtracted.

## 3. The states of the cell (one meaning each)

| State | Meaning | Cell face (Marketing's, Design measured at 1,024) |
|---|---|---|
| PRODUCED | filed count within the max age (section 4), no active-issuance flag (section 5), bound in range | "≤ 12.3M" |
| UNKNOWN | no filed share count exists for the name (or the dossier has no CIK, or the count is spread across several filings so no single count is defensible) | "Unknown" |
| CANNOT_VERIFY | a count exists but cannot be trusted as of today: older than the max age, OR active issuance (section 5), OR the bound fails its range check (not above zero, or above the count; the insider-only half cannot exceed the count, so that is a defect alarm) | "Cannot verify" |
| NOT_YET | nobody has computed it for this name yet | "not researched yet", never "Unknown" |

**Each cause has its own stored reason, so a popover never says a false one (Commercial 5e).** The surface prints the string for the stored reason, never one string for the state:

| State | Stored reason | True of |
|---|---|---|
| UNKNOWN | `NO_FILED_COUNT` | no filed share count exists for the name |
| UNKNOWN | `NO_CIK` | the dossier carries no SEC identifier, so no filing could be looked up |
| UNKNOWN | `COUNT_SPLIT_ACROSS_FILINGS` | the latest count's values come from several filings, so no single count is defensible |
| CANNOT_VERIFY | `COUNT_TOO_OLD` | the count is older than the max age at the checkpoint |
| CANNOT_VERIFY | `ISSUANCE_FLAGGED` | the dossier flags share issuance the count may not include (section 5) |
| CANNOT_VERIFY | `ISSUANCE_NOT_CHECKED` | no dossier exists as of the checkpoint, so issuance was not checked |
| CANNOT_VERIFY | `BOUND_OUT_OF_RANGE` | the arithmetic failed its own check (a defect alarm; a reconciliation fault, never "shares may have been issued") |

Marketing words each; a generic string is allowed only if it is true of every reason in its state.

Stored as an explicit state, never null, never zero. A bound of zero or below is CANNOT_VERIFY: insiders at 100 percent of the count is not a real company, it is a unit or basis error.

**Dates on the surface (Commercial item 7 and 5b):** the cell's "as of" is the FILED COUNT's date. Each subtracted holding's date, owner, form and accession go in the tooltip; the conditional ("when the inputs line up, the true float is at most this") goes in the popover. Both are Design and Marketing's to place.

## 4. The maximum filed-count age (Commercial's open ask)

**Proposal: 45 days, the existing constant `FILED_SHARES_CURRENT_DAYS` (`lib/research/float.ts:92`), reused, not a second number.** Its comment records the measurement: across 211 issuers (2026-08-21) the age of the count is bimodal with a valley, and anywhere in 30 to 90 days "gives the same coverage within three points"; 45 was chosen because a stale count that has issued shares lands hardest on the names that dilute hardest. Two measurements exist, of DIFFERENT populations, and they do not agree in shape. (1) The `float.ts` comment: 211 issuers on 2026-08-21, 46.9 percent within 45 days against 50.2 percent within 90 (a 3-point difference). (2) The short-interest calibration's committed raw (`short-interest-calibration/out/viewtime-raw-2026-09-15.json`), RE-DERIVED 2026-10-05 by `age-rederive.mjs` (output `age-rederive-out-2026-10-05.json`): 422 EVALUATED FINRA settlement-date pairs, ages as of each settlement date (Jul/Aug), NOT board checkpoints, 180 of them foreign; the count is the COMBINED source (the later of A and B). **No count: 74 of 422 (17.5 percent). A count: 348 (82.5 percent). Of ALL 422: age at most 45 days 171 (40.5 percent), at most 90 days 204 (48.3), at most 120 days 218 (51.7), at most 180 days 240 (56.9); older than 180 days 108 (25.6).** (Of the 348 with a count: 49.1, 58.6, 62.6 percent.) Domestic 242 pairs: 138 within 45 days (57.0 percent of the 242); foreign 180 pairs: 33 within 45 days (18.3 percent), 51 within 120. **66 of the 171 are counts dated on or after the settlement** (filed before the view date, so a settlement-based age is zero or negative; a board checkpoint cannot see that case, so the 45-day figure is probably a little generous for the board). The earlier "34, 46, 50 percent" figures in the sheet-removal plan and `integration.md:2120` do NOT reproduce from this file (46 and 50 are near 48 and 52 of the 90 and 120 rows; the 45-day row is 40.5, not 34); their derivation is unknown and they are retired. Do not fold UNKNOWN into CANNOT_VERIFY (my first draft did). By (2) at 45 days, before active-issuance refusals: about 40 percent could show a number, about 18 percent UNKNOWN, about 42 percent CANNOT_VERIFY on age. **A looser max age buys coverage (by (2), 90 days: +8 points; by (1), +3) at the price of printing "≤ X" against a count that can pre-date an offering.** I recommend 45 because the cell's only dangerous failure is a number that is too low, and age is the main route to it. The number is Integration's and his to move, and changing it is a one-line constant. Neither measurement is of the board's own population at its checkpoints; the validation prereg's amendment 1 measures that, at 45, 90 and 120, for $0, and that table is the trigger to revisit the choice.

**The 45-day maximum is LOAD-BEARING for the cell face (Commercial 5e item 7).** Commercial passes the count's date in the popover only while the maximum age stays 45 days and is ENFORCED AS A STATE (an older count is CANNOT_VERIFY, never a number). If he moves it to 90 or 120, the date returns to the cell face. So the age choice and the cell face are one decision, and Design should not build the face before the age is ruled. Wherever the number appears with no popover (report text, tool output, exports, any stored copy), it carries "as of {date}" inline.

Age is measured from the count's as-of date (cover-page date) to the CHECKPOINT's date, in whole days (floor, as `ageInDays`), not to the render time, so a row does not change state between the checkpoint and the page load.

## 5. Item 7: "Cannot verify" after active issuance (Commercial's rule adopted)

*Revised after Commercial's 5e (origin `4df63826`); the member list below was read in `lib/research/types.ts:331-345` and the calibration's own sets in `short-interest-calibration/calibrate-viewtime.ts:31-34`.*

**Why a rule at all.** A bound is "at most". Share issuance after the count's date makes the count LOW and the bound false, and the age gate alone does not catch a count 10 days old on a name issuing daily.

**Every `SupplyBlockType`, in or out, with the reason:**

| Type | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `ATM`, `EQUITY_PURCHASE_AGREEMENT` | IN, any date | continuous issuance: shares keep being created after any count |
| `WARRANTS`, `CONVERTIBLES`, `PREFERRED_CONVERSION` | IN, any date | exercise or conversion creates shares at any time |
| `RECENT_OFFERING`, `PIPE`, `ACQUISITION_SHARES`, `OTHER_ISSUED_SHARES` | IN **only if** the block's `effectiveDate` is after the count's as-of date, OR the block has no `effectiveDate` | a closed issuance is in the count only if it closed before the count's date; with no date we cannot show it did, so we refuse (the rule only adds refusals). The calibration used the same split (DISCRETE, with the date test) |
| `REGISTERED_RESALE` | OUT | registers the resale of shares that already exist (or arise under a conversion that has its own block); creates none |
| `SHELF_CAPACITY` | OUT | a dollar capacity, "capacity, not proof shares were issued" (`types.ts`); an offering made under it appears as its own block |

Commercial's 5e finding that the earlier list was narrower than the set is right; the three it named (`RECENT_OFFERING`, `PIPE`, `ACQUISITION_SHARES`) are in, with `OTHER_ISSUED_SHARES` added by the same reason.

**Which statuses trigger it:** `KNOWN_ACTIVE` and `POSSIBLY_ACTIVE`, not `NOT_YET_ACTIVE` or `UNKNOWN`. This WIDENS Commercial's rule (it named KNOWN_ACTIVE) to the calibration's own STRICT test, because the cell's only dangerous failure is a number that is too low and `POSSIBLY_ACTIVE` is the model saying it cannot rule issuance out. The cost is unmeasured and may be large (micro-caps often carry warrants), so Amendment 1 of the prereg reports the issuance row twice, KNOWN_ACTIVE alone and KNOWN_ACTIVE plus POSSIBLY_ACTIVE, and the wider rule stays only if that table shows the column is still usable; the choice is Integration's and his.

**The flag is a model's label, and its absence is not evidence (Commercial 5e).** `KNOWN_ACTIVE` is assigned by the interpreter (`schema.ts`) and guardrailed against the cited text (`guardrails.ts`; I have read these as grep lines, not whole files). So PRODUCED means "no issuance flag was raised in the desk's reading", never "no shares were issued", and no surface or the public page may say or imply the second.

**A name with no supply ledger.** (i) No dossier exists as of the checkpoint (never researched, or not yet): issuance was not checked, so the state is CANNOT_VERIFY with reason `ISSUANCE_NOT_CHECKED`. (ii) A dossier exists and its supply ledger is empty: the desk looked and raised no flag, so PRODUCED, under the reading above. I cannot tell from the type whether an empty ledger means "researched and none" or "research did not reach the supply section"; if the dossier carries a completeness marker for that section, the build uses it and treats a not-reached section as (i). I have not found one; Build confirms before the code is written.
