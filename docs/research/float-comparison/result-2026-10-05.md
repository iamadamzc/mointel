## What it shows

**1. Arm (a) has an unsafe half, and this is the finding that matters.** The bound was proposed as wrong only in the safe direction. That holds for the INSIDER family (direct, non-derivative common shares from Forms 3/4/5). It does NOT hold for the LARGE-HOLDER family: WCT's large holders total 102,800,000 shares against a filed count of 46,265,000, so the bound is negative. A 13D or 13G reports BENEFICIAL ownership, which by rule includes shares a holder can acquire (warrants, conversions) and so can exceed shares outstanding; WCT's count is also dated 2024-12-31, before its 2026-09-08 split. Either way, subtracting cover-page amounts can push the bound too LOW, the dangerous direction. **The large-holder family is not safe to subtract as designed.** Options: subtract only the insider family (looser, safe); or subtract a large holder only when its filing states shares held outright (not acquirable), which the structured cover page does not separate.

**2. Arm (a) without the large holders is usually close to the filed count.** In the table the bound subtracts the LARGER family, which was the large holders on SORA, SSM, WCT, CAMP and COLA. An insider-only bound (the safe half) would sit below the filed count on 4 names: NXL by 2.8%, SSM 10.0%, AISP 48.7%, CAMP 1.1%; on the other 6 it equals the filed count. On AMOD, HTCR and EJH every insider holding was refused for a split between the holding and the share count (the basis rule working as written).

**3. Arm (b) found a float for half the names, and 4 of those 5 are above the filed count.** A float cannot exceed shares outstanding on the same date and basis. The dates differ: (b)'s figures are dated 2025-06-30 to 2026-01-02, the filed counts mostly 2026-08. HTCR (15.5M as of 2025-06-30 against 1.5M filed 2026-08-13) and EJH (19.7M as of 2025-07-14 against 4.0M filed 2025-06-30) are far apart. **Whether a split falls between (b)'s date and the filed count's is NOT measured here:** the share-count basis frame came back "a bar in the span has no split factor" for 9 of 10 names, so the store could not answer it. As registered, the verdict stands (B_ABOVE_FILED); the frame is stated, not used to excuse it.

**4. The Sheet's floats, for reference:** on the 5 names it has, each is below (a)'s bound (SORA 4.86M under 10.4M; AMOD 594K under 5.0M; HTCR 660K under 1.5M; SSM 627K under 1.15M; EJH 2.46M under 4.0M), and each is far below (b)'s figure where both exist.
