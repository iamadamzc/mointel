## Three choices the design did not spell out, made here, for Integration's read

1. **A removal is recorded ON THE FIELD (`evidenceRisk.removed`: rule, shape, the removed sentence), NOT as a `Violation`.** Read in the code before building: any violation turns the whole dossier's outcome PARTIAL (`pipeline.ts`, `downgraded = validation.violations.length > 0`), the worker stores the job as PARTIAL (`worker/research.ts`, the status mapping), and `view.ts` can show PARTIAL_INTELLIGENCE; a REJECT buys a repair call and, still rejected, discards the whole new dossier. A removed risk sentence must cost none of that. For the same reason its ids are NOT run through `checkIds` (whose `SOURCE_ID_MUST_EXIST` is a REJECT): an id outside the bundle removes the sentence instead (`RISK_REQUIRES_SOURCE`). The removed sentence is stored for the audit of the removal rate (design section 4's known cost) and is never returned by `evidenceRiskState`.
2. **"May" before a number is the month, not the modal** ("effective on May 5, 2026", "filed in May 2026" survive). Without it the modal rule removes every sentence dated in May, which no list item intends. It only narrows a removal; Commercial reads it with the literal strings.
3. **Activity (guardrail 4) reads the CORRECTED ledger**, after `REGISTRATION_IS_NOT_SELLING` and `WARRANTS_REQUIRE_ELIGIBILITY` have downgraded statuses, so "KNOWN_ACTIVE" means what the stored dossier says.
