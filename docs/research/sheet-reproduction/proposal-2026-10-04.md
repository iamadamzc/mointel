### Option (a), recommended: the desk's own upper bound, from SEC filings

Designed 2026-09-04 (`docs/threads/research.md`, "HIS QUESTION: every float is invented"; Integration agreed it needs no fixture set), never built:

    float bound = filed shares outstanding  minus  holdings PROVEN to be affiliates

- **Subtract only what is proven:** officers, directors and 10%+ holders from Forms 3/4/5, 13D filers, and any holder at 10% or more whatever form they filed. A 13G holder under 10% stays IN the float. **Corrected 2026-10-05 (10-name test): the 13D/13G amounts are BENEFICIAL ownership, which includes shares a holder can still acquire, so subtracting them can take the bound below the true float (WCT: below zero). The rule's "safe direction" argument below holds for the Forms 3/4/5 direct-share half only.**
- **Why that rule:** every affiliate we fail to find makes the number HIGHER, which can only make a name look less tight than it is. The only way to be dangerous is to subtract someone we should not have, and the rule never does. So the true float is at most our number, and the board says so: **"no more than 4.2M shares, from SEC filings as of Sep 14"**. Below the desk's low-float line (10M, `LOW_FLOAT_CEILING`, `lib/research/float.ts:443`) that is a proof, not an estimate; above it, it says nothing and prints nothing more.
- **Coverage, measured earlier (item 23, 40 issuers):** Forms 3/4/5 exist for 98%, 13D/G for 70%; the limit is the denominator, a filed share count, on 80% (185 of 231). Where there is no share count the cell is UNKNOWN, never a guess.
- **The three ways it can go too LOW, and the guard for each** (all from the same 09-04 work): a share count that is stale under dilution (the age is carried and a stale one reads CANNOT VERIFY, not a number); a holding stated before a split subtracted from a count after it (the split-basis guard is already shipped in the supply pair); one holder counted twice across a Form 4 and a 13D (new: de-duplicate by filer CIK).
- **Source, for Commercial's allow-list:** SEC filings only, by construction; no web search, no vendor page. The stored source names each filing's accession number, so the cell can say "estimated from SEC filings" and every subtracted holding is auditable.
- **What it needs built:** Forms 3/4/5 are not fetched today (`lib/research/sec/forms.ts` lists 13D/13G and Form 144 but not 3/4/5); their XML is structured and parses deterministically. 13D/13G cover pages are the hard half; if that parse proves unreliable the bound falls back to shares outstanding minus Section 16 holders, which is looser but still a true upper bound. **No model call.**
- **Validation before it reaches the board** (pre-registered, no money): on a sample, check every subtracted holding is (1) an affiliate by the rule, (2) on the share count's split basis, (3) not counted twice, against the filing that states it. Commercial's measurement (researched float above filed shares) does not apply: the bound cannot exceed the share count by construction.

**What is new: one outcome row per call, computed by the desk, no model.** A "call" is a name on a checkpoint with a stored grade. After the NEXT session's regular close is latched, a deterministic job writes `desk_call_outcomes` (checkpoint, ticker, the call's reference price and basis, then from the next session):

| Outcome | Definition (stored values only) |
|---|---|

| Close | (next session's 16:00 close - reference) / reference |
| Retention | (close - reference) / (high - reference), the same formula as the narrative's F15 |
| Peak-to-close giveback | (high - close) / high |
| Peak rank | the best in-range rank the name held on any checkpoint of the next session; "not ranked" is its own state |
| Spike / fade | whether the next session's high printed in its first 30 regular-session minutes and the close finished under that minute's open; needs minute bars, so it is computed while they exist (they are pruned after the retention window) and stored |

Every outcome carries the N states: `NOT_YET` until the next session has closed and latched; `UNKNOWN` with a reason when an input is missing (no next-session row, a split between the call and the outcome, which reuses the narrative's split check). Never a zero for a missing value.

**Patterns: counts first, words only on approved thresholds.** A pattern is a grouping of calls by stored labels (grade A to D; each criterion's reading; Float Quality CLEAN / CAUTION / DIRTY_FLOAT / UNKNOWN; cycle). For each group the desk reports the sample size and the outcome distribution (median and spread of peak gain, retention, giveback; the share that ranked again). The recap's status words (NONE, EMERGING, ACTIVE, EXHAUSTION) are given thresholds ONCE, by him, before any status prints; until then the report shows counts and sample sizes only, so a small sample can never read as an edge. "Scalp-useful" has no definition in the prompt and no stored measure; it is left out unless he defines it.

**Scope line, kept exactly:** this measures the DESK's calls (its grades, its readings, its research), never his trades; it reads no account, order or position data.

**Where it shows:** nowhere on the board at first; it is analysis. Output is a periodic report of the patterns table (a doc or a Desk page, Design's choice) that he and the seats read.

**The legend trigger (recorded in section 6):** the first time any outcome test of the grades runs, the Priority legend's "not tested against outcomes" is changed in the same commit.

**Price and the box:** no model, no web, no new process; one SQL job after the next session's close in an existing worker (the assess service runs the checkpoint writer, `worker/desk-assessments.ts`, `deploy/aws/systemd/mointel-assess.service:28`), plus a small table that grows by at most the checkpoint's stored names (25 or fewer) per checkpoint. **$0 recurring.**
