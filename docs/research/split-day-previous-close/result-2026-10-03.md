## Answer

- **The defect is real.** On at least **12** split effective dates in the window (34 dates with latched regular-session values, 2026-08-17 to 2026-10-02), `ticker_session.previous_close` carried the PRE-split close onto the post-split day.
- **Source:** every one is `previous_close_source = 'alpaca-daily-bar'` with `baseline_adjustment = 'split'`. The baseline asked Alpaca for split-adjusted bars and still got the pre-split close. That fits the fetch-timing hypothesis (adjustment applied after the baseline was fetched), but the fetch time is NOT read: no `rvol_baseline` row matched `baseline_date = trading_date`.
- **None of the 12 reached the board or a report** in this window (0 checkpoint rows each, 0 report matches). Why not (band, eligibility, or the board reading another reference) is not measured.
- **The four registered "defects" that DID reach the board are detector false positives**, genuine moves (below). So no false split move printed on the board in this window, as far as this detector can see.

## Addendum: guard or luck (Integration's question), read from the code and the store

**Verdict: luck, not a split guard. Not contained.**

Why each of the 12 confirmed rows stayed off the board:

| Rows | What kept it off | A split guard? |
|---|---|---|
| TEUP, AXTU, CRWU, TTDU, CCUP (ETFs), IGR (fund) | `universe.discovery_eligible = 0` (instrument class) | No: an instrument filter that happens to cover them |
| RFAI | closed at $62.70, outside the band | No |
| BIAF, LRHC, WCT, TNMG, SFWL | eligible and in band, but relative-volume rank 1,945 to 2,815 and volume 7k to 197k shares | No: ranking by relative volume, and below the movers rule's 500k floor |

**What the board would have shown intraday.** Before the 16:00 bell the percent change is measured against the PRIOR close (`lib/market/derive.ts:44-50`, called at `lib/market/engine.ts:1639-1644`), and that close is the baseline's (`engine.ts:746-750`), so on these days it was the pre-split close. WCT on 09-08 would have read about +408% from premarket to the close. The stored `percent_change` (+2.6%) hides this: the row's last write was after the bell, when the reference rolls to today's close.

**Why the exposure is higher now.** The big-movers rule (`d1677f07`, 2026-10-01, in production `3b9c0a70` and `0b92c309`) adds in-band names moving at least `boardMoversMinPct` (10) from the prior close on at least 500,000 shares (`lib/market/config.ts:128-130`). It went live after every confirmed date. From now on, an in-band reverse-split name with 500k shares traded clears +10% on the false ratio gain and joins the board.

**A second surface, in reports v6 (not live):** the recap's counts (`lib/desk/reports/narrative.ts:61-103`) read `ts.previous_close` directly. On 09-08 its "ran at least 50 percent" count is 16 and includes SFWL, LRHC, AIXI, TNMG and WCT; on 08-24 it is 17 and includes BIAF (and BRCC, INCR, GAME are unverified). Those names also count as "up" in breadth.

**Fix sites, proposed, not built:**

Options: re-read the baseline's last daily bar after Alpaca has applied the day's corporate actions; or, when a split factor is known for the day (stored `daily_bars.split_factor`, or a corporate-actions read), restate `priorRthClose` by it; or, failing both, a basis check that marks the reference unverified (no percent change shown) when the session trades at least 1.8x away at a standard ratio.
2. The v6 recap reads the same corrected close, not `ts.previous_close` raw. Build note for the narrative work: F6 and F7 must not count a split name.
