halts from this field** — it mostly records that the desk does not know, and

absence of event.

**3. `cycle UNKNOWN` IS THE COMMON CASE, NOT THE EDGE CASE.** 6,766 `UNKNOWN`
and 2,220 `null` against 1,977 `YELLOW_FRONTSIDE_WARNING`, 451 `RED_BACKSIDE`
and 411 `GREEN_FRESH_FRONTSIDE`. **The "we don't know" register is the DEFAULT
voice, not a fallback for awkward rows.** *If the specimens only show confident
