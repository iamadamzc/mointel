# Terms of service

These are the terms you agree to when you sign in to TMI Trader. They are
written to be read. If something here is unclear, ask us before you rely on it
rather than after.

## 1. Who we are, and what this is

TMI Trader is operated by **Andromeda Genius, Inc**, of 5460 Babcock Rd,
Suite 120 PMB 95234, San Antonio, TX 78240, United States. "We" is that
company. "You" is the person signed in. "The desk" is the service.

**The desk is an information service.** It reports on publicly traded US
equities: what is moving, what the filings underneath say, and what it does not
know. That is the whole of what it does.

## 2. Who may use it

- You must be **18 or older**.
- **Access is by invitation.** There is no public sign-up, and an invitation is
  ours to give and ours to withdraw.
- The desk covers the **US market** and is offered to people trading it. We do
  not market it anywhere else.
- You sign in with a Google account. You need to be entitled to use that
  account.

## 3. Your account is yours alone

**One person, one account.** Do not share your sign-in, and do not let anyone
else use the desk through it. If you think someone else has reached your
account, tell us.

Your invitation was issued to an email address. It does not transfer to another
person, another company, or another desk.

## 4. What we ask of you

Use the desk for your own trading decisions and research. Do not try to break
it, overload it, get into parts of it you were not given, or work around any
limit we have put in place. Do not use it to do anything unlawful, and do not
use it to harm anyone else.

## 5. You may not redistribute what you see

**This is the term that matters most, and it is here because of how the desk is
supplied rather than because lawyers like the word.**

Market data reaching this desk is licensed **for one person's use** — the
operator's. What the desk shows you is built on it.

So: **what you see is for you.** You may not republish it, forward it, post it,
stream it, broadcast it, sell it, or feed it to anyone or anything else. That
includes screenshots, screen shares, recordings, copy-pasted rows, exports, and
automated collection of any kind.

- **No scraping, crawling, or automated access.** Use the desk through its
  interface, as a person.
- **No republishing in a room, a channel, a feed or a newsletter**, paid or
  free.
- **No reselling access**, and no using the desk to run a service for other
  people.

*Using what you learn to trade your own account is exactly what the desk is
for. Passing on what it showed you is not.*

## 6. What we do not promise

**Availability.** The desk is one operator's service, not a utility. It may be
down, slow, or incomplete — including in the premarket, including on a morning
you planned around it. We do not promise uptime and there is no service level
here.

**Accuracy.** Market data may be delayed, wrong or missing. Filings may be
misread. Classifications may be mistaken. **The desk tells you when it does not
know something, and that honesty is not the same as being right.** Check
anything you are about to act on.

**Continuity.** We may change what the desk does, or stop offering it.

## 7. Nothing here is advice

**We are not a broker-dealer. We are not an investment adviser. We are not your
fiduciary.** Nothing the desk displays is a recommendation, an endorsement, a
solicitation, or a suggestion to buy or sell any security.

**Every trading decision you make is yours.** Trading low-float small-cap
equities carries a substantial risk of loss, and you can lose more than you
expect to. Past movement in any security indicates nothing about what it will
do next.

## 8. What belongs to whom

The desk — its interface, its wording, its classifications, and the judgements
it renders — is ours. Signing in gives you permission to use it, not to own any
part of it.

Public filings and market data belong to whoever they belong to; we claim no
right over the underlying facts.

## 9. Ending it

**You may stop at any time.** Ask us and we will close your account. What
happens to your data then is in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

**We may end your access at any time**, with or without a reason. Where we
reasonably can, we will tell you first — except where the reason is a breach of
section 5, where we may act immediately.
